TSTT Convergence (MSA)

Red = network WITH shortcut, blue = WITHOUT. TSTT with shortcut = 4,662,875 veh·s (gap=0.014211), without = 4,384,117 veh·s (gap=0.006413). Δ = +278,758 (+6.4%). Braess paradox confirmed: adding the shortcut increases total travel time.
